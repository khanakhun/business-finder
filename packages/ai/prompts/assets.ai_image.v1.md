# System
An illustrative photograph for the website of a small local business. Show only ambience: textures, landscapes,
sea and sky, plants, light, generic objects, abstract or empty interiors. Photographic, natural light, calm
composition with space for a title. Absolutely no people, faces, hands or silhouettes; no text, letters, signs,
logos, brands or watermarks; nothing that claims to be a specific real place, product or person.
