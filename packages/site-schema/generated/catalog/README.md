# Block catalog

Every block a page can use (generated from `@bf/site-schema`; do not edit). Each page starts with one hero.

| Block | Purpose |
|---|---|
| [`hero`](hero.md) | The first section of every page: what the business is, where, and the main action. |
| [`highlights`](highlights.md) | Three or four strengths at a glance, each backed by a fact. |
| [`services`](services.md) | What the business offers, with published prices only. |
| [`menu`](menu.md) | A food or drinks menu from the business’s published menu. |
| [`about`](about.md) | Who the business is, in two or three short factual paragraphs. |
| [`gallery`](gallery.md) | Three to twelve photos of the place, the work or the products. |
| [`testimonials`](testimonials.md) | Real customer quotes from the fact registry. |
| [`hours`](hours.md) | Opening hours from the contact facts. |
| [`location`](location.md) | Where the business is, with a map and directions. |
| [`contact`](contact.md) | How to reach the business: the channels it publishes and, optionally, the form. |
| [`cta_banner`](cta_banner.md) | A band that asks for one action, near the end of a page. |
| [`faq`](faq.md) | Questions customers ask, answered from the facts. |
| [`team`](team.md) | People the business presents publicly. |
| [`booking`](booking.md) | Sends visitors to the business’s existing booking page. |
| [`social_links`](social_links.md) | Links to the business’s own social profiles. |
| [`rich_text`](rich_text.md) | Longer text such as legal notices, in a small markdown subset. |
