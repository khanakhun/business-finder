# Gallery (`gallery`)

Three to twelve photos of the place, the work or the products.

## Use it

- The business’s own or licensed photos, each with a real alt text.

## Avoid

- Fewer than three good photos (leave the block out).
- Stock photos that suggest something untrue.

## Variants

| Variant | When | Preview |
|---|---|---|
| `grid` | An even grid. | `thumbnails/gallery/grid.png` |
| `masonry` | Photos of mixed shapes. | `thumbnails/gallery/masonry.png` |
| `carousel` | One photo at a time, for many similar photos. | `thumbnails/gallery/carousel.png` |

## Props

| Prop | Type | Required | What to write |
|---|---|---|---|
| `heading` | text per locale |  | Section heading, e.g. "Our services". One entry per site locale, at most 80 characters each. |
| `assetIds` | list (3–12) of string | yes | Three to twelve image assets. |

## Examples

- Good: Alt: The terrace facing the bay / Alt : La terrasse face à la baie
- Bad: Alt: image1.jpg / Alt : photo
